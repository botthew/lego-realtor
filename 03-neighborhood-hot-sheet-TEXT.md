# Neighborhood Hot Sheet
## The Insider's Guide to Your Neighborhood

---

## How to Use This Guide

This is your cheat sheet for understanding a neighborhood BEFORE you move.

For each neighborhood you're considering, fill out this guide. Then compare side-by-side. You'll know exactly where your money goes and what lifestyle you're getting.

## Resources for Neighborhood Research

### Websites
- **GreatSchools.org** — School ratings & data
- **NeighborhoodScout.com** — Demographics, crime, walkability
- **Walkscore.com** — Walkability scores
- **CrimeReports.com** — Crime data by neighborhood
- **Zillow / Redfin** — Home prices, sales history
- **Nextdoor.com** — Neighborhood social network
- **Google Maps** — Explore street view, find amenities

### In-Person Research
- [ ] Visit neighborhood at different times (morning, evening, weekend)
- [ ] Walk the streets (feel the vibe)
- [ ] Talk to current residents (stop for coffee, chat at park)
- [ ] Visit local coffee shop (listen to what people talk about)
- [ ] Attend community event (if timing allows)
- [ ] Call local police non-emergency line (ask about neighborhood)
