## Don't Miss Anything in Your Final Week

## Day 1: Received Closing Disclosure

### What This Is
A 3-page document that shows:
- Final loan amount
- Interest rate
- Monthly payment
- All closing costs (itemized)
- Who pays what
- Lender and title company fees

### What to Do
1. **Read it carefully** (yes, all 3 pages)
2. **Check numbers match pre-approval:**
   - Loan amount correct?
   - Interest rate correct?
   - Down payment amount matches what you've saved?
3. **Closing costs line up?**
   - Appraisal: $400-600 ✓
   - Title insurance: $500-1,500 ✓
   - Attorney: $500-1,500 ✓
   - Taxes/insurance (prepaid): should be reasonable ✓
4. **Any surprises?** Call lender immediately
5. **You must wait at least 3 business days before closing** (legal requirement)

### Red Flags
- ❌ Loan amount suddenly higher than pre-approval
- ❌ Interest rate 0.5% higher than promised
- ❌ Closing costs jumped $5K+ unexpectedly
- ❌ Monthly payment way off

**If you see red flags:** Call your lender's closing department NOW. Don't wait.

## Day 2: Secure Final Funding

### Get Wire Instructions FROM YOUR LENDER
Do NOT wire money based on email instructions alone. Too many fraud scams.

**Safe process:**
1. Call your lender's main number (from their website, not email)
2. Ask: "Can you confirm wire instructions for my closing?"
3. Get bank routing number, account number, reference
4. Call YOUR bank and confirm those details are legitimate
5. Set up wire transfer

### Amount to Wire
Closing disclosure tells you exact amount:
- Down payment: $X
- Closing costs: $Y
- **Total: $X + $Y**

**Add $500 cushion** (for last-minute adjustments)

### When to Wire
- Day of closing is typical
- Some attorneys ask for day before
- **ASK your closing attorney when to wire**

### Document Everything
- Screenshot wire confirmation
- Save wire reference number
- Your bank gives you a wire receipt
- Forward confirmation to closing attorney

**Do NOT rely on "someone will get the money." YOU confirm it.**

## Day 3: Confirm Homeowners Insurance

### You Cannot Close Without This

Insurance requirements:
- **Proof of homeowners insurance** (binder)
- **Coverage amount** = at minimum your loan amount
- **Coverage includes:** dwelling, personal property, liability
- **Lender is listed as loss payee** (they get paid if house burns)

### Get a Quote
1. Contact 2-3 insurance companies
   - State Farm, Allstate, Amica, AAA
   - Or use broker (Insurify, NerdWallet)
2. Provide:
   - Home address
   - Square footage
   - Year built
   - Roof age
   - Heating type (gas/electric)
3. Compare quotes (should be $800-2,000/year for typical DMV home)
4. Pick your agent

### Get the Binder
- Insurance agent issues a "binder" = temporary proof of coverage
- Covers you until policy is fully issued
- Valid for 30-60 days
- Email binder to your closing attorney ASAP

### Bring to Closing
- Binder document (they may ask to see it)
- Final insurance policy (if available)

## Day 4: Final Home Inspection & Walkthrough

### Schedule Final Walkthrough
**When:** 24 hours before closing (legally required in most states)

**Who:** You + your real estate agent

**How long:** 30-45 minutes

**What to Bring:**
- Home inspection report (from Phase 3)
- Copy of your contract
- Checklist (see below)
- Phone to take photos if issues

### Final Walkthrough Checklist

**Agreed repairs completed:**
- [ ] Check every repair from inspection negotiation
- [ ] Are they done properly? (or quick fix?)
- [ ] If NOT done, get written explanation from seller

**No new damage:**
- [ ] Ceiling stains (new leaks?)
- [ ] Wall damage
- [ ] Broken windows/doors
- [ ] Scratches on floors
- [ ] Missing items

**Agreed items still there:**
- [ ] Light fixtures (if included in sale)
- [ ] Appliances (if included)
- [ ] Garage door openers
- [ ] Window treatments (if agreed)
- [ ] Washer/dryer (if agreed)

**Utilities working:**
- [ ] Lights (all rooms)
- [ ] HVAC (heat/AC on)
- [ ] Water (hot + cold)
- [ ] Gas (if applicable)

**If You Find Issues**
1. **Take photos/video**
2. **Text your agent immediately**
3. **Get written response from seller within 4 hours**
4. **Options:**
   - Seller fixes before closing (rare)
   - Escrow $ for repair (you fix after)
   - Renegotiate closing price down
   - Walk away (only if major issue + no contingency left)

**Most Common Issues:**
- Agreed repair not complete (get 48 hours to fix or escrow)
- New damage found (document it, ask for credit)
- Item was removed (light fixture, appliance—get credit)

### After Walkthrough
- Text agent final decision: "Ready to close" or "Need seller to fix X"
- Forward any issues/photos to attorney
- Attorney communicates with seller's attorney
- Resolve before next day

- [ ] All addenda/amendments
- [ ] Inspection report
- [ ] Appraisal report
- [ ] Pre-closing walkthrough photos

### Review Closing Statement (Closing Disclosure)

One more time:
- [ ] Loan amount = pre-approval? ✓
- [ ] Interest rate = agreed? ✓
- [ ] Down payment = accurate? ✓
- [ ] Monthly payment calculated correctly? ✓
- [ ] Closing costs = expected? ✓
- [ ] Property tax estimate reasonable? ✓
- [ ] Insurance estimate reasonable? ✓

If anything looks wrong, email attorney today. Don't wait until closing day.

### Contact Your Attorney (Or Title Company)

Email them:
1. Confirmation of wire amount + timing
2. Closing time/location
3. Where to park
4. Who to ask for when you arrive
5. What to bring
6. Any last-minute issues from final walkthrough
7. Estimated time closing will take

### DO NOT Make Large Transactions

- [ ] No new credit card purchases
- [ ] No new loans
- [ ] No job changes announced
- [ ] No large bank transfers
- [ ] Keep accounts stable

One weird transaction can trigger underwriter re-review. Not worth it.

## Day 6: Last-Minute Prep

### Confirm Final Details

**Call your lender:**
- "Closing is tomorrow. Final loan amount is $___. Interest rate is __%. Is everything still on track?"
- Ask: "Anything else you need from me?"

**Email your attorney:**
- "Confirming final closing time is ___. I'll bring ___. Wire will arrive ___."

**Text your agent:**
- "See you tomorrow at closing!"

### Final Financial Check

**Wire amount confirmed:** $________

**Money available:** ✓
- In your checking account? ✓
- Can you wire it? ✓
- Wire limit with your bank? ✓ (call bank if limit too low)

**Cashier's check/wire receipt ready:** ✓

### Set Up Auto-Pay (Optional But Smart)

Set up automatic mortgage payment before you close:
- Less stress managing payments
- Never miss a payment
- Builds good payment history

Most lenders will set this up during closing, but you can ask to do it yourself.

## Day 7: CLOSING DAY

### Morning Of

**Eat breakfast.** You'll be at closing for 1-2 hours. You'll want energy.

**Dress professionally** (not required but nice for photos)

**Leave 20 minutes early** to account for traffic

**Bring everything** on your list above

**Bring your phone** (may need to verify information)

### What Happens at Closing

**Step 1: Meet the team** (5 min)
- Attorney/title officer
- Lender rep (sometimes virtual)
- Your agent
- Sometimes seller + their agent present

**Step 2: Review documents** (20 min)
- Attorney walks through all papers
- Explains what each one does
- Answers questions
- You take notes

**Step 3: Sign, initial, and date** (30-45 min)
- Closing disclosure (3 pages)
- Promissory note (your promise to repay)
- Mortgage document (lender's security)
- Deed (proof of ownership transfer)
- Title commitment
- Various other forms (lender, insurance, HOA stuff)
- **You'll sign ~80-100 times**

**Step 4: Wire or check** (5 min)
- Confirm wire was received
- Or provide certified check
- Get receipt

**Step 5: GET KEYS** (5 min)
- Seller's agent hands over keys
- You're the owner!
- Take photos with keys
- Celebration time

### Common Closing Questions

**Q: Why are there SO many pages to sign?**
A: Lender requirements, title company requirements, HOA documents. Most are repetitive. It's normal.

**Q: What if I don't understand something?**
A: ASK. The attorney is there to explain. Don't sign anything you don't understand.

**Q: Can my spouse/co-buyer not attend?**
A: Usually both are required. If one can't attend, attorney may require power of attorney. Ask ahead.

**Q: Will the seller be there?**
A: Not always. Their attorney signs on their behalf and returns documents.

**Q: How long does this actually take?**
A: 1-2 hours typical. Plan 2 hours to be safe.

**Q: What if something goes wrong at closing?**
A: Very rare. But if underwriter finds issue: closing gets delayed 1-3 days while resolved. Unlikely but possible.

## Common Last-Minute Issues (And Solutions)

### "The seller won't make the repair."
- **Solution:** Escrow = attorney holds money for 30-60 days while you get bids and repairs done. Then attorney releases $ to you for repair.

### "Title insurance company found a lien on the property."
- **Solution:** Lender won't close until lien is paid off by seller. Seller's attorney handles. Delay 2-5 days typical.

### "Appraisal came back too low."
- **Solution:** 
  - If you're desperate: pay difference in cash
  - If not: renegotiate price with seller
  - If seller won't: walk away (with contingency)

### "Homeowners insurance is more expensive than quoted."
- **Solution:** Get emergency quotes from other carriers. Title company/attorney can work with lender on temporary binder while you shop.

### "Utilities aren't transferring to my name."
- **Solution:** Not usually a closing blocker, but call utilities to confirm they're set to transfer on closing day.

### "I'm getting cold feet."
- **Solution:** Normal. Talk to your agent about why. Most regrets are pre-closing jitters, not real problems. Sleep on it.
