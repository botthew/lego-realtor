# 🧱 The Lego Realtor

**Making real estate fun and understandable**

## About The Brand

The Lego Realtor is a personal brand dedicated to breaking down complex real estate concepts into simple, visual, and engaging content. Using LEGO as a creative metaphor, we make buying, selling, and investing in real estate accessible to everyone.

## 🎯 Content Pillars

### 1. Real Estate Education
Breaking down complex real estate concepts using LEGO visualizations - from mortgages and interest rates to market cycles and investment strategies.

### 2. Home Buying Guides
Step-by-step visual guides to the home buying process, making it less intimidating for first-time buyers.

### 3. Market Insights
Visual explanations of market trends, data, and news - because understanding the market shouldn't require a finance degree.

### 4. Behind The Bricks
Behind-the-scenes content showing how we create our LEGO real estate content and the stories behind the builds.
