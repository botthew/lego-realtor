## Part 3: Funnel Strategy & Lead Flow

### Lead Capture Flow

```
Content Post (CTA)
    ↓
Landing Page (Lead Magnet Preview)
    ↓
Email Capture Form
    ↓
Email Sequence (3-5 emails over 7 days)
    ↓
Consultation Offer / Upsell
    ↓
Sales Conversation
```

### Email Sequence Examples

#### Sequence 1: "Blueprint" Optin
1. **Email 1 (Immediate):** Deliver magnet + quick win
2. **Email 2 (Day 2):** "What comes next?" — pre-approval primer
3. **Email 3 (Day 4):** Social proof — client success story
4. **Email 4 (Day 7):** Limited offer — free consultation call

#### Sequence 2: "Checklist" Optin
1. **Email 1 (Immediate):** Deliver checklist + bonus tips
2. **Email 2 (Day 1):** Most missed items (urgency)
3. **Email 3 (Day 3):** Real estate agent Q&A
4. **Email 4 (Day 5):** "Close faster" tips + consultation offer
