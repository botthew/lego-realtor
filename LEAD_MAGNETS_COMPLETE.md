## Expected Conversion Metrics

### Download Rate
- **Target:** 5-10% of landing page visitors
- **Formula:** Downloads ÷ Landing page visits = Download %
- **Example:** 100 visits → 5-10 downloads

### Email Open Rate
- **Target:** 20-30% of email recipients
- **Formula:** Opens ÷ Emails sent = Open %
- **Example:** 100 emails → 20-30 opens

### Click-Through Rate
- **Target:** 3-5% of emails opened
- **Formula:** Clicks ÷ Opens = CTR
- **Example:** 20 opens → 1-2 clicks

### Lead to Consultation Rate
- **Target:** 10-20% of leads book consultation
- **Formula:** Consultations booked ÷ Leads = Consultation %
- **Example:** 50 leads → 5-10 consultations
