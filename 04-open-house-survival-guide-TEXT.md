# Open House Survival Guide
## How to Spot Problems & Stand Out When Buying

---

## Why This Guide Matters

Open houses are your chance to see a home in person. But 30+ people walk through in 2 hours. You need to be strategic.

This guide teaches you:
1. What to inspect (and why)
2. What questions to ask
3. What red flags mean
4. How to take notes so you remember it
5. How to follow up effectively

---

## Pre-Open House Prep (Before You Go)

### 1. Do Your Homework

**Research the home:**
- Visit Zillow / Redfin listing
- Note key features mentioned
- Check google street view of surrounding area
- Read agent's description (note what they emphasize)
- Look at past sales history (was it owned long? How many sales?)

**Know what the owner wants:**
- If there's an asking price, note it
- Are there multiple offers mentioned?
- Days on market (quick sale = competitive area)

### 2. Check Comps Beforehand

Visit Zillow and search for:
- Similar homes in neighborhood (beds, baths, sq ft)
- What they sold for in last 3 months
- What's asking price vs. sold price
- Price per sq ft (helps you evaluate)

**Why:** If home is listed at $500K but similar homes sold for $450K, it's overpriced. You'll negotiate from this understanding.

### 3. Plan Your Timing

**Best times to visit:**
- **Weekday morning** (9-11 AM) — fewer people, you can ask questions
- **NOT Saturday 11 AM-1 PM** — everyone's there, madness, can't focus
- **If you must go on weekend:** Early (9-10 AM) or late (4-5 PM)

**Why:** You can actually see the home and talk to the agent.

### 4. Gather Your Tools

**Bring:**
- [ ] Notebook & pen (take notes immediately, don't rely on memory)
- [ ] Phone (for photos, voice memos, measurements)
- [ ] Measuring tape (to verify listing square footage)
- [ ] Flashlight (to check dark corners, attic, basement)
- [ ] Business cards (to give to agent if you're serious)
- [ ] Pre-approval letter (leave with agent if showing interest)

**Optional:**
- Moisture meter (check for water damage in basement)
- Outlet tester (test if outlets work)
- Tape measure app (for quick measurements)

## Open House Checklist: What to Inspect

### Exterior (Before You Enter)

**Roof condition:**
- [ ] Shingles sagging or curled? (Bad = $15K+ replacement)
- [ ] Moss/algae growth? (Sign of moisture/age)
- [ ] Missing shingles?
- [ ] Age estimate: How old does it look?

**Gutters & downspouts:**
- [ ] Sagging or loose?
- [ ] Clogged with leaves/debris?
- [ ] Missing sections?
- [ ] Do they drain water AWAY from house?

**Foundation:**
- [ ] Large cracks? (Fine cracks normal, cracks >1/8" = concern)
- [ ] Water stains on foundation?
- [ ] Horizontal cracks? (RED FLAG = structural issue)
- [ ] Bowing walls?

**Siding & exterior:**
- [ ] Paint peeling?
- [ ] Siding damaged or rotting?
- [ ] Windows: Broken, rotting frames, gaps?
- [ ] Door jambs rotting?

**Landscaping:**
- [ ] Trees too close to house? (Can damage foundation)
- [ ] Vegetation against siding? (Moisture, pests)
- [ ] Grade sloping toward or away from house? (Away = good)

**Driveway & walkways:**
- [ ] Cracked, potholed asphalt?
- [ ] Steep slope (ice/snow hazard)?
- [ ] Well-maintained?

**Photos to take:**
- Entire front exterior
- Any damage noted above
- Roof condition (use zoom)
- Foundation view
- Siding close-up if issues

### Entry & Foyer

**Door & frame:**
- [ ] Solid or flimsy?
- [ ] Locks working?
- [ ] Frame intact?
- [ ] Moisture damage around frame?

**Floors:**
- [ ] Hardwood, laminate, or carpet?
- [ ] Gaps between boards? (Can indicate settling/moisture)
- [ ] Squeaky? (Normal but note it)
- [ ] Stains or discoloration?

**Walls:**
- [ ] Paint fresh or dingy?
- [ ] Holes patched or left?
- [ ] Water stains on ceiling/walls?
- [ ] Wall cracks?

**Lighting:**
- [ ] Natural light? (Windows, skylights)
- [ ] Artificial light? (Bright or dim)
- [ ] Does foyer feel welcoming?

### Living Room / Main Areas

**Floors:**
- [ ] Condition (same notes as foyer)
- [ ] Covered or visible hardwood?

**Walls:**
- [ ] Cracks?
- [ ] Water stains?
- [ ] Musty smell? (Mold indicator)

**Windows:**
- [ ] Open easily?
- [ ] Seals intact or separated? (Separated = moisture between panes, needs replacement)
- [ ] Locks work?
- [ ] Single, double, or triple pane?
- [ ] Natural light adequate?

**Ceiling:**
- [ ] Water stains or discoloration?
- [ ] Popcorn ceiling (contains asbestos if pre-1980s)?
- [ ] Cracks or sagging?

**Fireplace (if present):**
- [ ] Functional or just for decoration?
- [ ] Chimney inspected recently?
- [ ] Damper works?
- [ ] Cracks in brickwork?

**Smell test:**
- [ ] Fresh & clean?
- [ ] Musty / moldy?
- [ ] Pet odors? (Hard to remove)
- [ ] Cooking smells? (Normal)
- [ ] Chemical smells? (Masking something?)

### Kitchen

**Cabinets:**
- [ ] Solid wood or cheap veneer?
- [ ] Doors close properly?
- [ ] Hinges tight?
- [ ] Water damage on bottom (sink leaks)?
- [ ] Interior condition clean?
- [ ] Hardware original or updated?

**Countertops:**
- [ ] Material (granite, quartz, laminate, tile)?
- [ ] Condition (cracked, stained, worn)?
- [ ] Functional or cosmetic issue?

**Appliances:**
- [ ] Included in sale? (Ask agent)
- [ ] Age & condition?
- [ ] Refrigerator (runs, no rust)?
- [ ] Range (burners work, oven heats)?
- [ ] Dishwasher (runs, leaks)?
- [ ] Microwave functional?
- [ ] If old/failing: Budget $3K-8K for replacements

**Sink & plumbing:**
- [ ] Faucet works? (Hot & cold water)
- [ ] Leaks under sink? (Check cabinets)
- [ ] Disposal functional?
- [ ] Water pressure decent?

**Electrical outlets:**
- [ ] Plenty of outlets?
- [ ] Some appear new, others old? (Possible rewiring needed)
- [ ] Any burned outlets or discoloration?

**Lighting:**
- [ ] Bright, adequately lit?
- [ ] Natural light from window?

**Overall condition:**
- [ ] Clean and well-maintained?
- [ ] Cosmetic updates or original 1970s?
- [ ] Is it a cooking kitchen or just functional?

### Bathrooms

**Toilet:**
- [ ] Flushes properly?
- [ ] Seat secure?
- [ ] Any cracks in bowl?
- [ ] Water runs continuously? (Leak)

**Sink:**
- [ ] Faucet works?
- [ ] Hot & cold water?
- [ ] Drain drains quickly or slow?
- [ ] Vanity solid or cheap?

**Shower / Tub:**
- [ ] Clean or moldy?
- [ ] Caulk intact or separating? (Separating = water damage)
- [ ] Grout cracked or missing?
- [ ] Pressure adequate when you test it?
- [ ] Water temperature consistent?

**Ventilation:**
- [ ] Exhaust fan works? (Turn it on, listen for suction)
- [ ] Or window for ventilation?
- [ ] Mold in corners? (Sign of poor ventilation)

**Floor & walls:**
- [ ] Water damage, soft spots on floor?
- [ ] Mold or mildew?
- [ ] Grout discolored?
- [ ] Caulk separating from tub/shower?

**Lighting:**
- [ ] Adequate?
- [ ] Natural window or just artificial?

**General:**
- [ ] Spotless or dirty? (Cleanliness = owner care indicator)
- [ ] Odor present?
- [ ] Age of fixtures (original or updated)?

### Bedrooms

**Flooring:**
- [ ] Condition, damage?
- [ ] Carpet or hardwood?
- [ ] If carpet, stains or odors?

**Walls:**
- [ ] Cracks?
- [ ] Water stains?
- [ ] Fresh paint or dingy?

**Windows:**
- [ ] Working properly?
- [ ] Natural light?
- [ ] Locks function?

**Closets:**
- [ ] Adequate space?
- [ ] Ventilation?
- [ ] Moisture or smell?

**Ceiling:**
- [ ] Water stains?
- [ ] Sagging?
- [ ] Cracks?

### Basement / Crawl Space

**Foundation walls:**
- [ ] Cracks? (Large = bad)
- [ ] Bowing?
- [ ] Water stains or efflorescence? (White mineral deposits = moisture)

**Moisture:**
- [ ] Dry or damp smell?
- [ ] Water marks on walls? (Indicates flooding history)
- [ ] Sump pump present? (If in wet area, should have one)
- [ ] Dehumidifier running? (Sign of moisture problem)

**Electrical:**
- [ ] Panel/breaker box labeled clearly?
- [ ] Signs of recent upgrades?
- [ ] Age estimate?

**Mechanical:**
- [ ] Furnace age & condition?
- [ ] Water heater age? (Should be < 15 years)
- [ ] Ductwork intact or damaged?

**General:**
- [ ] Finished or unfinished?
- [ ] Headroom adequate?
- [ ] Used for storage or potential living space?
- [ ] Proper egress (safe exit) if finished?

### Attic

**Access:**
- [ ] Can you safely access?
- [ ] Insulation present?
- [ ] Amount: 6", 12", none?

**Roof structure:**
- [ ] Water stains on rafters/sheathing? (Roof leak)
- [ ] Dark stains = mold
- [ ] Ventilation adequate? (Ridge vents, soffit vents)

**Age indicators:**
- [ ] Recent roof work visible?
- [ ] Original sheathing or replacement?
- [ ] Asbestos insulation (pre-1980, fluffy, pink)?

### Mechanical Systems

**HVAC (Heating / Air Conditioning):**
- [ ] Unit runs when you turn it on?
- [ ] Hot air blows when heat is on?
- [ ] Cold air blows when AC is on?
- [ ] Age estimate? (Should last 15-20 years)
- [ ] Recently serviced? (Ask agent)

**Electrical panel:**
- [ ] Up to code (100+ amp)?
- [ ] Properly labeled breakers?
- [ ] Any double-breaker situations? (Bad sign)
- [ ] Age estimate?

**Plumbing:**
- [ ] Water pressure adequate?
- [ ] All drains work?
- [ ] Visible rust or corrosion?
- [ ] Copper (good) or galvanized (oxidizing) pipes?

**Water heater:**
- [ ] Type (gas, electric, tankless)?
- [ ] Age? (Should be <15 years)
- [ ] Rusting or leaking?
- [ ] Proper venting?

## Questions to Ask the Agent (Or Seller)

Have these ready on your phone. Ask confidently:

**General:**
1. "How long has this home been on market?"
2. "Are there other offers?"
3. "What's the seller's motivation?" (Job change, upgrade, downsize?)
4. "Has this home had recent inspection?"
5. "What are the annual property taxes?"
6. "Are there any HOA fees?"
7. "When was last major renovation?" (Roof, HVAC, plumbing, electrical)

**Specific to issues you found:**
8. "I noticed [water stain on ceiling]. What caused that?"
9. "When was the roof last inspected/replaced?"
10. "How old is the HVAC system?"
11. "Has the plumbing ever needed major repair?"
12. "Is there a history of basement flooding?"
13. "Have pests ever been an issue?" (Termites, roaches, mice)
14. "What's the utility cost average?" (Get winter & summer)

**Negotiations:**
15. "Would seller consider covering closing costs?" (Especially if issues found)
16. "Is there flexibility on closing date?"
17. "What's the earliest possible closing?"

## Red Flags: When to Walk Away

**Serious issues (consider passing):**
- ❌ Water damage or mold (expensive, health risk)
- ❌ Significant foundation cracks (structural = $10K+)
- ❌ Recent roof failure (need $15K+ replacement)
- ❌ Electrical hazards (fire risk, rewiring expensive)
- ❌ Failing HVAC + old furnace (replace = $8K+)
- ❌ Bad odors that suggest buried problems
- ❌ Evidence of pest infestation (hard to fix)

**Ask your inspector, but consider passing.**

**Yellow flags (negotiate or investigate):**
- ⚠️ Old but functional HVAC (may last 5 more years)
- ⚠️ Cosmetic damage (paint, caulk, trim)
- ⚠️ Worn carpet (replace = $3K-5K, normal)
- ⚠️ Outdated kitchen (cosmetic, your choice)
- ⚠️ Small water stains (if isolated, likely just roof leak)
- ⚠️ Basement moisture (sump pump + dehumidifier can manage)

**These are negotiable. Don't kill the deal unless price is already high.**

## After the Open House: Your 24-Hour Analysis

**Within 24 hours:**

1. **Review your notes** (while memory is fresh)
2. **Look at your photos** (identify any issues you missed)
3. **Compare to comps** (is this priced right?)
4. **Discuss with your agent** (get their professional opinion)
5. **Calculate potential costs:**
   - Inspection: $300-500
   - Appraisal: $400-600
   - If major repair needed: $$?
   - Total repair budget: ?
6. **Decide: Am I interested?**
   - If yes → Move to inspection
   - If no → Attend next open house

## If You Want to Make an Offer

**Within 48 hours of deciding:**

1. **Schedule professional home inspection**
   - Your condition contingency gives you 10-14 days
   - Get inspection within first 5-7 days
   - Review report thoroughly
   - Hire inspector who attends closing (for follow-up questions)

2. **Get pre-approval letter** (shows you're serious)

3. **Research comparable homes** (for negotiating)

4. **Prepare your offer:**
   - Price (based on comps, condition, your budget)
   - Down payment percentage
   - Earnest money deposit
   - Contingencies (inspection, appraisal, financing)
   - Closing date

5. **Submit offer** (typically through agent)

6. **Be ready to negotiate** (seller may counter)

## Pro Tips for Standing Out at Open House

### If You Love the Home

1. **Tell the agent you're serious:**
   - "I'm pre-approved and ready to move fast"
   - Leave your pre-approval letter
   - Leave your contact info

2. **Be professional:**
   - Dress decently (shows respect)
   - Don't criticize the home while there
   - Don't bring 5 people (looks desperate)
   - Don't stay too long (others want to tour)

3. **Ask good questions:**
   - Shows you've done your homework
   - Agent respects educated buyers
   - You sound serious, not just browsing

4. **Follow up same day:**
   - Email agent: "Loved the home. Would like to discuss offer."
   - Speed + seriousness = advantage

### If You're Not Sure

1. **Schedule a follow-up private showing**
   - "Can I come back Tuesday evening for a second look?"
   - Less crowded, more time to think

2. **Bring your agent**
   - Gets expert opinion on issues
   - Can answer your questions
   - Gets home inspected sooner if needed

3. **Take time to research**
   - Sleep on it
   - Check comps
   - Talk to trusted people
   - Don't rush
